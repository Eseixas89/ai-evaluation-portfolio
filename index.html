03Workflow · Evidence review

A source-grounded
research workflow

A repeatable way to move from research inputs to an inspectable brief.

Problem
Unstructured research can mix weak sources, repeated coverage, and claims that are hard to trace.
My role
With AI assistance, I organized gathering, source filtering, synthesis, drafting, and human review into five stages with explicit handoffs.
Result
Five documented validation examples with source ledgers, findings, and human-review notes. The published time-saving figures are estimates, not measured productivity gains.
